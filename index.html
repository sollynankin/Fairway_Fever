How to play

Pick a club with ↑/↓ or click it. Distances shown are for your current lie, wind and elevation.
Aim with ←/→ (hold, or Shift, for bigger steps; up to 90° either way) to fight crosswind or cut a corner.
Swing — 3 clicks (Space or mouse): 1st starts the aim indicator swinging up to ±7.5° around the angle you set, 2nd locks it and starts the power bar, 3rd locks power (full bar = 100%).
Pick a club from the scrolling bar above the bars (distances include wind, slope and lie).
Aim with the ◀ ▶ buttons (hold to move faster, up to 90°).
Swing with the big yellow button, 3 taps: SWING starts the aim indicator swinging up to ±7.5° around your angle, LOCK AIM locks it and starts the power bar, LOCK POWER takes the shot (full bar = 100%).
The ☰ button has the caddie, green view, distance markers and camera.
Rough and bunkers rob your distance (rough won't bounce, fairway will); water & out-of-bounds cost a stroke. Aim off-centre and you risk a chunk (right of centre) or a top (left) - safe inside 1°. The ground rolls: slopes change how far it plays, kick bounces sideways, and sloped lies change your launch (uphill = higher, ball above feet = pulls left).
